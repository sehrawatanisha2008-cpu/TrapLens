Cyber Awareness Guide

Learn how scam websites gain trust.

Most scams do not begin with advanced hacking. They begin with psychology: urgency, fear, fake rewards, and trust manipulation.

1. Urgency Pressure

Scammers use phrases like “limited time”, “verify now”, “account blocked”, or “offer expires soon” to stop users from thinking clearly.

2. Fake Rewards

Free phones, lottery winnings, huge discounts, and prize claims are common tricks used to make users click unsafe links.

3. Fake Login Pages

Phishing pages often copy the design of trusted brands but use slightly different URLs to steal passwords or personal data.

4. Payment & OTP Traps

Real companies do not ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, or card PINs through random websites or messages. These are major warning signs.

5. How to Stay Safer

Check the full URL, avoid rushed decisions, verify the source, search for reviews, and never share sensitive information on unknown websites.
